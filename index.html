Private pool studio · North Georgia

A cinematic underwater film. Of you.

One session, in a private saltwater pool, shot on cinema cameras — the same way I shoot wildlife and the cinematic underwater films I make of people, now turned on you.

I light it, direct it, and shoot it, then it's cut into a finished film.

It's made for people who want an artistic, personal piece of themselves, and for performers and musicians who need footage that doesn't look like everyone else's.

You don't need to be a strong swimmer. The pool is private and saltwater — gentler on skin and eyes than a typical chlorinated pool — and I've spent years working underwater on a single breath. The water is my job, not yours.

The session

One flat rate. One finished film. No packages, no menu, no “contact for pricing.”

  • About a half-day — two to four hours in the water
  • A finished hero film, cut and color-graded
  • A short vertical edit for social
  • A handful of stills pulled from the footage
  • Delivered within four weeks
